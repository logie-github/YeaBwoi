# Wrath Remix: the story

The story Wrath Remix tells, quest by quest: who gives each quest, what you do, and where it sends
you next. Every step leads to the next one; nothing is left to find on your own. The build follows
this script (the SQL in `data/sql/remix/world/`, the scenes in `src/RemixNpcs.cpp`); where the two
disagree, this file is what was meant.

WRM-nnn is quest 77nnn. Original quests are named with their ids.

## The premise

You are a hero of Azeroth's present day, after the Shadowlands and the war beneath the earth.
Eternus of the Infinite Dragonflight has pulled you out of your own time and into Northrend in the
year the Lich King's war begins. The journey costs travellers their strength: you arrive as a
level 10 with nothing but what Eternus gives you, and you grow strong again as the war goes on.
That is what a Timerunner is.

Eternus wants the war to go differently. The war against the Lich King cost the world dearly:
Saurfang's son and Bolvar Fordragon at the Wrathgate, the Forsaken's betrayal there, the dead of
every battle raised to fight for the Scourge. Eternus believes a kinder history is possible, and
that heroes who know how the story goes can steer it there. The bronze dragonflight would call
this a crime. Eternus answers that the bronze also let Stratholme burn.

He is not lying to you about what he wants. He is wrong about what it costs.

In Wrath itself the Infinite Dragonflight is already the enemy of history: they try to stop the
Culling of Stratholme, and Chromie hunts their agents at Wyrmrest Temple. Eternus is one of them
who believes he is better than the rest.

## The people

- **Eternus** (Infinite Dragonflight): the Bazaar's master. Grand, patient, sincerely grieving for
  the people the war will kill. He never admits he is wrong; after the turn he keeps trying to
  save the war's dead, and his agents fight you for it.
- **Moratari** (Infinite Dragonflight): Eternus's lieutenant and your guide. A scholar of what
  she calls variables, dry and curious. She believes in Eternus until Sylvanas gives her a reason
  to look, and then she looks.
- **Sylvanas Windrunner**, from the future, after her sentence began. The bronze dragonflight
  brings her back, because no one alive understands the Lich King's place in the world better:
  she served the Jailer who made his Helm, and she is spending eternity freeing the souls it took.
  If the war goes differently, those souls are lost, and the Helm passes to no one fit to hold
  it. She also stands to gain more than anyone from a different war: Arthas killed her and made
  her a banshee. That she will not let it change is what makes her believable. She keeps out of
  her own sight: the Sylvanas of this time is alive in the Undercity, and must never see her.
- **Alleria Windrunner**, from the future: her sister, a ranger touched by the Void, who can feel
  when the world's shape is wrong. Wry where Sylvanas is cold; she came because Sylvanas asked,
  which Sylvanas does not do. (The 3.3.5 game has no Alleria; she wears the look of the living
  Ranger-General, which is what she looked like before the Void.)
- **Chromie** (bronze dragonflight), at Wyrmrest Temple, as the game has her: she is already
  fighting the Infinite there (Mystery of the Infinite). She brought Sylvanas and Alleria.
- **Andormu and Nozari** (Keepers of Time): arrive at the Bazaar after the turn, with the bronze.
- At the Bazaar throughout: Alurmi (Bronze trader, sells to anyone), the Bronze vendors, Zephyr's
  roads, Sa'at's dungeons, the Chronologist, Momentus (Infinite Power), the class instructors.

## The Infinite Bazaar

The home of the whole story, in Crystalsong Forest beneath Dalaran, west of the Violet Stand. Every
new Timerunner wakes here (no more Caverns of Time). Your hearthstone remembers it. Until the turn
it is Eternus's camp; after it, Eternus is gone from it and Sylvanas and Alleria are there, with
Moratari and the Keepers of Time.

## Prologue: the Infinite Bazaar (level 10)

1. **WRM-010 Out of Time** (Moratari, beside you as you wake → Eternus, a few steps away). Moratari
   tells you where and when you are, that Eternus brought you, and why you are weaker than you
   remember: travelling through time costs strength, and it comes back. She hands you over to
   Eternus.
2. **WRM-011 What the War Will Cost** (Eternus → Eternus). Eternus shows you three echoes of the war
   he means to change, frozen moments standing around the camp: a footman of Valiance Keep who
   will die on the beach and rise again for the Scourge; Dranosh Saurfang at the Wrathgate; Bolvar
   Fordragon in the dragonfire. Speak with all three, then with Eternus: his offer.
3. **WRM-012 Time's Toll** (Eternus → Moratari). Moratari explains what you lost and how to get it
   back: Bronze, earned for everything you do; Alurmi, who trades heirloom gear that grows with
   you; your class's instructor, who teaches what you've forgotten. Speak with both, then with
   Moratari. Your hearthstone now remembers the Bazaar.
4. **WRM-013 A Small Demonstration** (Moratari → Moratari). Time pushes back against being
   changed, and the seams fray: anomalies come through. Moratari lets three through, one at a time;
   you deal with them.
5. **WRM-014 / WRM-015 To the Front** (Moratari → Eternus; the Alliance's, the Horde's). Eternus sends you to the war's first day. Both
   sides land in the Borean Tundra: the Alliance at Valiance Keep, the Horde at Warsong Hold. Eternus
   folds the road, and Moratari meets you at the landing, by the officer enlisting volunteers.

Death knights keep Acherus and their own story; when the Ebon Blade lets them go, Moratari finds
them, tells them what they missed, and brings them to the Bazaar at their chapter.

## Chapter I: The Northrend Invasion (Borean Tundra, 10 to 20)

One route for everyone: the Borean Tundra. Howling Fjord stays in the world for whoever wants it.

The game's own chains, with a step added wherever the game leaves you to find the next quest giver
yourself: a short quest from the last quest's giver to the next one ("Report to..."). Moratari's
transitions skip the afternoons of errands, as before, but now they are Eternus's: he is
shortening the war, and you are helping.

- **Alliance:** Enlistment Day, A Time for Heroes, The Siege, Death From Above, Plug the Sinkholes;
  Leryssa's search for Thassarian (WRM-006 *A Useful Omission*, Eternus's: he skips the road to
  Thassarian), Thassarian, the Death Knight, Finding the Phylactery, Buying Some Time, Words of
  Power, Last Rites.
- **Horde:** Hellscream's Vigil, The Defense of Warsong Hold, Taking Back Mightstone Quarry, Cutting
  Off the Source, Wind Master To'bor, Magic Carpet Ride, Tank Ain't Gonna Fix Itself (WRM-016 *A
  Useful Omission*: the tank's parts), Tanks a lot..., The Plains of Nasam.
- **WRM-017 / WRM-018 A Kindness Out of Time** (mid-chapter, by Sergeant Hammerhill after Plug the
  Sinkholes, or by Overlord Razgor after Cutting Off the Source): Eternus's first "improvement".
  Moratari brings his request: a squad history lost (the 7th Legion's on Valiance Keep's beach, a
  Warsong patrol in Mightstone Quarry's tunnels); clear the crypt crawlers or the Nerub'ar before
  they go in, and they come home. You do. It feels like heroism. (Sylvanas will later ask what
  became of the ones who would have taken their places.) The chain's next quest opens after it.
- **WRM-019 Word at the Gate** (Alliance, Moratari → Mark Hanes): Word on the Street is his, a little
  way off, and the game leaves it to be found.
- **WRM-121 Into the Dragonblight** (by the chapter's last quest giver): Eternus wants you at the
  Dragonblight's front. To Wintergarde Keep or Agmar's Hammer.

## Chapter II: The Wrathgate (Dragonblight, 20 to 32)

The chapter the story turns on.

1. Arrival, and the faction's chain begins (Alliance: Naxxramas and the Fall of Wintergarde, Horde:
   Victory Nears...).
2. **WRM-201 A Summons from Wyrmrest** (Moratari, after the first chain quest): the Wyrmrest Accord
   has asked for you by name. That should not be possible. To Wyrmrest Temple.
3. **Mystery of the Infinite** (12470, Chromie, the game's own): Infinite agents attack Chromie's
   hourglass; you defend it, and your own future self comes to help. Then Chromie introduces the
   two women she brought back with her.
4. **WRM-202 Strangers Out of Time** (Sylvanas, at Wyrmrest): Sylvanas and Alleria, from the future.
   Sylvanas tells you to stop: whatever Eternus has promised, the Lich King's story has to happen
   as it did, to the letter. You return to the Bazaar; Eternus answers that the woman lecturing you
   about preserving history will one day burn a world tree, and asks you to keep going.
5. The faction chain, and **WRM-203 The Road to Angrathar** (II-A, Eternus's): the camp's errands
   skipped, as before.
6. **WRM-204 A Warning, Unwritten** (Eternus): Eternus's real plan. The Forsaken will betray everyone
   at the Wrathgate; he asks you to stop it before it happens (the Alliance: carry a warning to
   Bolvar's lieutenant at Fordragon Hold; the Horde: spoil the Apothecary Society's batch at
   Venomspite). You set it in motion.
7. **WRM-205 The Weight of Time** (Sylvanas, at the Bazaar): Sylvanas and Alleria walk into the
   Bazaar. Sylvanas tells Moratari what happens if Bolvar does not burn: no one takes the Helm
   after Arthas, and the Scourge has no master and no leash. Moratari listens, casts a spell and
   vanishes. Ten seconds later a portal opens and she steps out of it: she has seen it. Sylvanas is
   telling the truth, and the war has to happen as it did. Eternus knows it too, and refuses: he
   leaves, and takes his loyal Infinite with him. From now on the Bazaar is Moratari's, Sylvanas's
   and Alleria's.
8. **WRM-206 Undo It** (Moratari): stop what you started. Eternus's agents carry your warning (or
   guard the spoiled batch); fight them and put it right.
9. **WRM-207 The Hour of the Gate** (Sylvanas, at Angrathar): Eternus sends his agents to the
   Wrathgate to break the charge. Fight them off, so that history happens.
10. **Return To Angrathar** (12499 / 12500) and the Wrathgate, as the game has it.
11. **WRM-208 What We Cannot Change** (II-B, Moratari and Sylvanas, by Alexstrasza): no skip. Sylvanas
    on Putress: he was hers, she did not order this, and she remembers learning of it.
12. The post-Wrathgate chains and the Battle for the Undercity, as the game has them (Sylvanas
    watches her past self take back her city, from where she can't be seen).
13. **WRM-221 After Angrathar** (II-C, Alleria): to the Grizzly Hills and Chapter III.

## After the turn: the transitions

From Chapter III on, a transition is no longer Eternus skipping history for you. It is Eternus's
agents changing it, and you stopping them. Each one is a fight at the place where the original
quests would have been (Infinite agents at the chapter's levels), then the block they tried to
change is safe and Moratari takes you on to the next scene. The original quests are always still
there to play.

- **Chapter III** (Grizzly Hills): *A Shorter Path Through the Woods* (after Ursoc: Infinite agents
  trying to warn Drakuru away), *A Friend of Questionable Judgment* (at Drak'Tharon's door, no
  skip), *The Truth About Drakuru* (to Zul'Drak).
- **Chapter IV** (Zul'Drak): *The Things the Scourge Remembers*, *The Paperwork of Betrayal* (the
  Infinite inside Voltarus), *A Pilgrim's Shortcut*, *The Gods Are Still Silent*, *A Kingdom Gone*.
- **Chapter V** (Sholazar Basin): *A Brief Hunting Expedition*, *The Waygate Is Ready*, *The Makers'
  Trail*.
- **Chapter VI** (the Storm Peaks): the mine and the pit fights (VI-A), the Halls of Lightning
  (VI-C), *Storm's End*.
- **Chapter VII** (Icecrown): the guide's VII-A, VII-B (Bridenbrad), VII-C (the Hall of Memories).

## The end (the user's design)

1. **Eternus's last stand**, at the doors of Icecrown Citadel, before the raid: Eternus and the
   Infinite Dragonflight make a last attempt to stop you. You win. Eternus is not killed: at 10%
   health he retreats through a rift.
2. **Icecrown Citadel**, the raid as the game has it: the Lich King's fight and the ending
   cinematic are untouched.
3. After the cinematic, you are brought back to the Bazaar, and **See You Later** begins (Moratari):
   tell her you're ready to go back to the future, then say goodbye to the people who matter
   (Sylvanas, Alleria, the Keepers, the Bazaar's people). Moratari tells you that because of what
   happened here, and because time is unreliable, some people may never know what you did; and that
   to protect what comes after, she will wipe this whole thing from Sylvanas's and Alleria's
   memories.
4. **Their stories**: before you go, you can talk with Sylvanas and Alleria about what comes after
   for them, expansion by expansion, as people talk, not as a list: Sylvanas through the Cataclysm
   (the Forsaken's war for Gilneas, the val'kyr), Pandaria, Draenor, the Legion and the Broken
   Shore, her war against the night elves and Teldrassil, Lordaeron, the Shadowlands, her
   sentence, the Dragon Isles and the war beneath; Alleria through the Twisting Nether and the
   Army of the Light, Argus, the Void, Telogrus and the ren'dorei, the Shadowlands, Dornogal and
   Xal'atath. Where one of them wasn't there, what they would say from where they were, without
   contradicting the lore. Alleria's part in the whole story is as large as Sylvanas's.
5. **Back to your own time**: talk to them or skip it, then tell Moratari you are ready. As Legion
   Remix does, your Timerunner goes to the ChromieCraft realm: at the level you reached, with what
   you carry (anything that isn't Wrath's own, which there shouldn't be, is turned into Wrath gear
   of the same kind and strength; Bronze stays behind). The game closes and the server restarts;
   you log in on ChromieCraft in your side's capital, Stormwind or Orgrimmar.
