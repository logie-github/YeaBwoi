Download WoW Vanilla addons for 1.12.1 private servers, including Turtle WoW: quest helpers, raid tools, and UI mods, free to install.

Vanilla WoW runs on client version 1.12.1, and it is the version almost every classic-era private server is built on, including Turtle WoW and its custom content. This page lists WoW Vanilla addons made for that client, covering both the base 1.12.1 experience and server-specific extensions.

Why Vanilla WoW needs addons

The original 1.12.1 interface predates most of the quality of life tools players expect today. There is no quest tracker on the map, no damage meter, and no way to see which party member needs a decurse or dispel without watching the raid frames closely. Addons cover these gaps without touching the game’s balance or rules.

Custom servers add their own content on top of the base client. Turtle WoW, for example, adds new zones and quests through its Mysteries of Azeroth expansion, and a few addons, such as pfQuest Turtle, are built specifically to support that extra content. Check which server an addon is made for before installing it, since a build made for one server will not always show correct data on another.

Addon categories on this page

The addons here are grouped by function, so you can filter by what you need instead of scrolling through the full list:

  • Quest helpers that mark quest givers and objectives on the map
  • Combat and damage meters for dungeons and raids
  • Bag and inventory organizers
  • Auction house tools for pricing and scanning
  • Unit frame and nameplate replacements
  • Gathering trackers for herbs, ore, and fishing nodes
  • Class-specific addons for cooldowns and buff tracking

Use the filters at the top of this page to narrow the list by category, server, or update date.

Popular addons for Vanilla WoW

Questie adds a quest tracker to the map, showing quest givers, objectives, and turn-ins, which the base 1.12.1 client does not provide on its own.

Titan Panel adds a control bar to the top or bottom of the screen and supports plugins for gold tracking, durability, coordinates, and more.

Decursive lets you remove curses, poisons, and diseases from party and raid members with a single click instead of targeting each player manually.

AuctioneerSuite scans the auction house and tracks price history, so you can tell whether an item is priced fairly before buying or selling.

Bagnon combines your bags and bank into a single window and adds search and sorting.

DPSMate tracks damage and healing done during combat, useful for checking raid performance without relying on a full combat log parser.

Gatherer marks herb, mining, and fishing nodes on your map and minimap as you collect them.

How to install Vanilla WoW addons

  1. Download the addon as a .zip file from its page on Warperia.
  2. Extract it with 7-Zip, WinRAR, or the extractor built into Windows.
  3. Open your WoW installation folder, then Interface, then AddOns.
  4. Move the extracted folder into AddOns. The folder containing the .toc file needs to sit directly inside AddOns, not nested inside another folder.
  5. Start the game and click the AddOns button on the character selection screen to confirm the addon is enabled.

Some private servers block addons last updated for a different client version. If an addon does not appear after installing it, tick “Load out of date AddOns” on the same screen.

Keeping addons up to date

Vanilla addons still get updated by their authors, either to fix bugs or to add support for server-side content patches. An outdated addon is one of the most common causes of Lua errors on login, so if something breaks after a server update, check this page or the app for a newer version before assuming the addon is broken for good.

Frequently asked questions

Do Vanilla addons work on every private server?

Most do, since they are built against the 1.12.1 client itself rather than a specific server. A few addons are made or patched for one server’s custom content, such as pfQuest Turtle for Turtle WoW. These are labeled on their addon page.

Can I use TBC or WotLK addons on Vanilla?

No. Addons built for later expansions use a different API version and will not load on 1.12.1. Look for addons that list 1.12.1 or Vanilla as their supported client, which is the case for everything on this page.

Why do I get a Lua error after logging in?

This usually means an addon is out of date for the current client or server patch, or that two addons are conflicting with each other. Disable your addons one at a time to find which one is causing the error, then check for an updated version.
